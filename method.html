Lymphatic Drainage

リンパドレナージュ

Overview

A delicate, precise touch that encourages sluggish lymph flow.

繊細で的確なタッチで、滞ったリンパの流れを促します。

Effects

Eases swelling, supports the release of waste, gentle detox.

Recommended For

Full-body heaviness or swelling, and those who run cold.

Shiatsu

指圧

Overview

A traditional technique applying sustained pressure to deep muscle and pressure points, releasing tension at its source.

筋肉やツボへ持続的な圧をかけ、コリを根本からほぐす伝統技法です。

Effects

Relieves deep-seated tension, improves circulation, supports nervous system balance.

Recommended For

Stubborn shoulder or lower back tension, and deep fatigue.

Botanical Oil Treatment

Recommended For

Dry skin, and those seeking a deeper sense of calm.

Foot Reflexology

フットリフレクソロジー

足裏の反射区に働きかけ、全身のバランスを整えます。

Relieves foot fatigue and swelling, supports internal function.

Recommended For

Those on their feet often, or fatigued from travel.
