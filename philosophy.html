The Philosophy of TENNE

Not force. The depth a body can accept.

力ではなく、身体が受け入れる深さへ。

The Meaning Behind TENNE

天と手

Heaven and hand.

TENNE takes its name from two Japanese characters: 天 (ten), and 手 (te).

天 means heaven — height, stillness, a time to step away from daily life and return to yourself. 手 means hand — human warmth, the sense of reading what the body cannot yet put into words, and the craftsmanship refined over years of practice.

Together, they hold our intention: to meet each body by hand, and offer only what it truly needs.

天と手を重ね、その人に本当に必要な施術を届けたい。その想いを込めています。

Pressure That Listens

Not force. Precision.

強さではなく、精度。

Not applied — absorbed.

Strong pressure doesn't always mean deeper release. When the body is tense and met with sudden force, it instinctively guards itself — the surface yields, but tension often remains held beneath.

TENNE isn't about changing the body through force. A hand rests quietly, reading breath and muscle response, to find the depth a body can naturally accept — pressure absorbed rather than pushed in. Depth, direction, rhythm, and duration are adjusted continuously, waiting for tension to release in its own time.

Not strength. Precision. That is what depth means at TENNE.

圧を押し込むのではなく、一人ひとりの身体に溶け込ませるように。

Every Body is Different

No two bodies. No two sessions.

同じ身体はなく、同じ施術もありません。

The body changes daily. Posture, sleep, work, travel, exercise, stress, weather — countless factors shape how tension, swelling, breath, and fatigue show up. Even for a returning guest, today's session is rarely identical to the last.

So TENNE never repeats a fixed routine. Before each treatment, we check in with how the body presents that day — reading tension, imbalance, and breath through touch, in ways words alone can't capture. Where fatigue has gathered. What's restricting movement. How much depth the body can accept. Each session is built, piece by piece, for that day's body alone.

その日の身体のためだけに、施術を組み立てます。

The TENNE Method

Many techniques, one continuous flow.

複数の技術を、一つの流れへ。

  • Lymphatic Drainage リンパドレナージュ
  • Shiatsu 指圧
  • Botanical Oil Treatment オイルトリートメント
  • Hot Stone Therapy 温石
  • Foot Reflexology フットリフレクソロジー
  • Slimming Leg Wrap Treatment 美脚ラップ
  • Head Spa ヘッドスパ

Techniques honed over more than a decade of practice are combined to suit the body in front of us.

Where swelling has settled, an approach that encourages flow. Where deep tension remains, gradual pressure guided by the body's response. Where movement feels restricted, shiatsu. When even the head and nerves won't rest, head spa.

Rather than treating each technique separately, touch and rhythm flow continuously into one session. Not fitting the body to a fixed course — building the session around the body. That is what bespoke means at TENNE.

決められたコースに身体を合わせるのではなく、身体に合わせてセッションを構成します。

Beyond Relaxation / Crafted by Hand

Only a hand can know this.

手だからこそ、分かることがある。

TENNE offers more than a pleasant hour. Accumulated tension releases, breath deepens naturally. A body that felt heavy moves with more ease. Swelling and stiffness soften, revealing the body's natural line. And beneath the pressures of daily life, even the mind quietly unwinds.

The warmth at the moment of touch. Muscle tension shifting with each breath. Small responses the body can't put into words. A hand can sense these subtle changes, adjusting pressure and pace within a single session.

With over a decade of hands-on experience — all-hand body sculpting, lymphatic care, body shaping, head care, foot reflexology, and Seitai — every technique has been refined into one practice of Japanese bodywork. A precision distinct to Japan. A care that doesn't compromise on detail. A handcraft that meets each body, quietly. TENNE brings that value to clients around the world.

日本ならではの繊細さと、手仕事の技術を、世界のお客様へ届けていきます。

"A treatment shaped by your body.
A precision felt beyond pressure."

あなたの身体のためだけに組み立てる、ただ一つのセッションを。
