Your Experience

ご利用の流れ

A journey shaped by quiet hospitality.

静かなおもてなしが紡ぐ、ひとつの旅。

From the moment you arrive to the last sip of tea, every detail at TENNE is considered — so the only thing left for you to do is rest.

Hospitality, Considered

Hospitality is care that goes unnoticed.

おもてなしとは、気づかれないほどの心配りです。

What TENNE values isn't visible performance, but quiet attentiveness — the way a bag is set aside, the room's temperature and lighting, the pacing of conversation before and after a session. Each is small on its own, but together they build a time you can truly relax into.

A comfort that needs no words. That's what hospitality means at TENNE.

言葉にしなくても伝わる心地よさ。それが、TENNEの考えるホスピタリティです。

The Flow of Your Visit

ご来店からお見送りまで

Arrival to aftercare, in one unhurried motion.

  1. 01

    Arrival & Welcome

    お出迎え

    You are greeted quietly and shown straight into your private room. Coats and bags are taken care of, and a moment is given to simply arrive — no forms to rush through, no waiting room to sit in.

    静かにお出迎えし、そのままプライベートなお部屋へご案内します。書類に追われることも、待合室で待つこともありません。

  2. 02

    Changing & Settling In

    お着替え・お部屋へ

    A private changing area, soft linens, and a quiet room set to a comfortable temperature. Lighting is kept low; the space is yours alone for the duration of your visit.

    プライベートな更衣室と、心地よいリネン。照明を落とした静かな部屋を、ご滞在中はあなただけの空間としてご用意します。

  3. 03

    Private Consultation

    個別カウンセリング

    Once settled in your room, a brief, unhurried conversation about how your body feels today — posture, tension, fatigue, and anything you'd like the session to focus on. Nothing is assumed in advance.

    お部屋で落ち着いたところで、今日の身体の状態について短く丁寧にお伺いします。姿勢、緊張、疲れなど、決めつけずにお聞きします。

  4. 04

    The Treatment

    施術

    Composed around what your body needs on the day, adjusting depth, direction, and rhythm throughout — precision over pressure, felt rather than announced.

    その日の身体に合わせて、深さや方向、リズムを細かく調整しながら進めます。強さではなく、精度で。

  5. 05

    Aftercare Tea & Guidance

    アフターティー・お見送り

    A quiet moment with tea before you return to the day, along with simple guidance for the hours and days that follow — how to move, rest, and hold onto the ease you've found.

    お茶とともに静かなひとときを。その後の過ごし方について、簡単なアフターケアもご案内します。

The Space

空間へのこだわり

Quietness, designed.

静けさも、設計されています。

Lighting kept neither too bright nor too dim. The sound of the air, the scent in the room, the texture of the linens — every detail is set to let the body unwind naturally.

We deliberately limit how many guests we welcome each day, leaving space between appointments so nothing ever feels rushed — a promise of fully private time.

時間に追われる感覚のない、完全にプライベートな時間をお約束します。
