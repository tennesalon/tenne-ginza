About the Therapist

セラピストについて

Emma

Owner & Bodywork Therapist

With over 10 years of hands-on bodywork experience, spanning from deep relaxation to body sculpting, I have dedicated my career to listening closely to each client's unique physical needs.

施術歴10年以上。リラクゼーションからボディスカルプティングまで、一人ひとりの身体と向き合ってきました。

View More +

By assessing posture, muscle tension, swelling, breath, and overall body alignment, each custom-tailored session gracefully blends lymphatic drainage, shiatsu, stretching, oil treatment, foot reflexology, and head spa.

My philosophy centers on precision over force. By understanding the deep connections within the body and meticulously adjusting the direction and rhythm of every stroke, I aim to create a profound sense of release and restore effortless movement. I am honored to bring the delicacy, hospitality, and handcraft of Japanese bodywork to clients from all over the world.
